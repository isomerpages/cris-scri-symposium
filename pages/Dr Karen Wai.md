	<h4><b>Dr Karen Wai</b></h4>
<b>Chief Strategy Officer<br>
Aevice Health</b>
	
<p>Dr Karen Wai is a healthcare executive and physician with over 25 years of international experience spanning clinical research, medical devices, digital health, and commercialization. She currently serves as Chief Strategy Officer at Aevice Health, a respiratory medtech company focused on advancing remote monitoring solutions for asthma and other chronic respiratory disease management.</p><p>

Prior to Aevice, Dr Wai held senior leadership roles at IQVIA and other healthtech companies, where she oversaw regional real-world evidence, clinical operations, digital health innovation, and AI-driven healthcare solutions across Asia Pacific. She has contributed to more than 80 clinical studies and has extensive experience in clinical trial strategy, site networks, patient engagement, and commercialization of healthcare technologies.</p><p>

Dr Wai is a frequent speaker, moderator, and advisor on clinical research, digital health, and healthcare innovation, with experience spanning multinational corporations, start-ups, and venture-building initiatives across the healthcare ecosystem.</p>
