<h4><strong>Registration begins in February 2026</strong></h4>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Symposium Passes</strong>
</p>
<p>The first three ticket categories offer access to the SCRI Clinical Trials
Symposium only (30–31 July 2026).
<br>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>All-Access Pass</strong>
</p>
<p>Access to one Pre-Symposium Workshop (28–29 July 2026) + the Symposium
(30–31 July 2026).
<br>
</p>
<p><strong>Important Registration Notes</strong>
</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The Standard Pass applies to private sector participants, doctors, and
individuals not eligible for the other discounted categories.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Delegates residing in Lower or Middle Income Countries/Regions may qualify
for a further discounted rate. Select the Standard Pass, and the adjusted
rate will be automatically applied once your country/region is selected
during registration.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>The All-Access Pass includes one Pre-Symposium Workshop (28–29 July 2026)
and the Symposium (30–31 July 2026).</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Bulk discounts are available for groups of 10 or more delegates. Please
contact <a href="mailto:projects@csquare-creative.com" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">projects@csquare-creative.com </a> for
details.</p>
</li>
