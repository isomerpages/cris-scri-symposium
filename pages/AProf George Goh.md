	<h4><b>Assoc Prof George Goh</b></h4>
<b>Associate Professor<br>
Singapore General Hospital, Duke-NUS Medical School</b>
	
<p>Prof George Goh is a Senior Consultant Gastroenterologist at Singapore General Hospital, with appointments as fellow of Academy of Medicine, Singapore (FAMS) and fellow of the American Association for the Study of Liver Disease (AASLD), Clinical Associate Professor, Duke-NUS Medical School and Clinical Assistant Professor, National University of Singapore. He is currently Director of the Clinical Trials Research Centre in Singapore General Hospital, which oversees clinical trial conduct in the institution. With over 15 years of clinical trial experience, he remains actively involved in advisory board committees as well as Principle Investigator in many multicenter clinical trials and research related to hepatology.</p><p> Prof Goh has published in the fields of viral hepatitis, fatty liver, liver cirrhosis, hepatocellular carcinoma and organ transplantation, and has been an invited faculty at local, regional and international conferences. He is also subspecialty editor for Singapore Medical Journal and on editorial boards of international journals like Clinical Gastroenterology &amp; Hepatology, Alimentary Pharmacology &amp; Therapeutics and Journal of Clinical &amp; Translational Hepatology.
</p>
