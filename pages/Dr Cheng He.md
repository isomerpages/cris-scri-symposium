	<h4><b>Dr He Cheng</b></h4>
<b>Vice President of Research and Development<br>
MiRXES
</b>
	
<p>Dr Cheng is the Vice President of Research and Development at MiRXES, a biotechnology company dedicated to the early detection of disease through microRNA-based diagnostics. MiRXES has been a pioneer in translating RNA biology into clinically actionable tools, particularly in the area of cancer screening.</p><p>
One of their flagship innovations is the GASTROClear test, a blood-based diagnostic that enables the detection of gastric cancer at early stages—when intervention can significantly improve patient outcomes. This represents a major step forward in shifting cancer management from late-stage treatment to early detection and prevention.</p><p>
Originally trained in materials science, Dr Cheng brings a multidisciplinary perspective to biomedical innovation. After being invited by the company’s co-founders to join MiRXES, he recognized the urgent clinical need for early cancer detection and has since played a central role in building the company’s R&amp;D capabilities. Under his leadership, MiRXES has helped demonstrate the transformative potential of RNA biology in medicine - even before the global spotlight on RNA technologies brought about by mRNA vaccines.
</p>
