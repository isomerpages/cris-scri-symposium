	<h4><b>Ms Wendy Loo</b></h4>
<b>Clinical Trials Ambassador<br>
ESTHER Ambassador</b>
	
<p>As a patient and caregiver, Wendy appreciates the importance of patient advocacy in public healthcare systems. She volunteers as a Clinical Trials Ambassador <i>(Clinical Trials Ambassador Programme under Clinical Trials Research Centre, Singapore General Hospital)</i> and ESTHER Ambassador with Singapore General Hospital <i>(ESTHER Ambassador under ESTHER Network Singapore and SingHealth Centre of Person-Centred Care)</i>.</p><p>
Wendy started her career in banking and logistics MNCs leading regional strategic planning, treasury management, and business development functions. She contributed to Singapore’s growth as a premier Biomedical Hub by providing clinical trial logistics solutions to pharmaceutical and central laboratory organisations.</p><p>
After 15 years in the private sector, Wendy fulfilled her passion to serve her community by joining Singapore’s public sector in 2002. Over two decades, she served in various Singapore’s economic and social services agencies, leading strategic planning, industry and manpower development, and grant management functions. Wendy often led initiatives that were transformational in nature and enjoyed engaging community and industry partners to co-create inclusive and impactful solutions.</p><p> 
She combines her love for travel with photography.
</p>
