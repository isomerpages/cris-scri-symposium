	<h4><b>Dr Han-Hui Cheong</b></h4>
<b>Clinical Development Operations Country Liaison (ASEAN)<br>
Boehringer Ingelheim Singapore</b>
	
<p>Dr Han-Hui Cheong is a Clinical Development Operations Country Liaison at Boehringer Ingelheim, with close to 20 years of experience across clinical research, healthcare, regulatory, and quality compliance settings. In her current role, she supports country and site feasibility for Boehringer Ingelheim clinical trials across Southeast Asia, working closely with investigators, institutions, and regulators to align study implementation with local needs and patient priorities.</p><p>
Her background spans hospital practice as a Principal Research Pharmacist, academia as a Research Fellow, and global quality compliance, giving her a well-rounded perspective on the clinical trial landscape. She also supports the Clinical Trial Ambassador Program, promoting greater awareness and understanding of clinical research among healthcare professionals and the community.</p><p>
She is passionate about demystifying clinical trials and strengthening collaboration across academia, regulators, and industry to advance patient-centric research and meaningful clinical innovation.
</p>
