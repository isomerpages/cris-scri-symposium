	<h4><b>Ms Nabilah Rahman</b></h4>
<b>Senior Statistician<br>Singapore Clinical Research Institute (SCRI)</b>
	
<p>Ms Nabilah is a senior statistician at the SCRI Biostatistics team. </p><p>

Ms Nabilah specialises in health services research and clinical trials. She has over 10 years of experience in study design and statistical analyses, of which 9 years were in public healthcare institutions. Ms. Nabilah has supported studies in various disease areas, including but not limited to diabetes, oncology, maternal health, and infectious diseases. She has taught data visualization in R, introduction to study designs and biostatistics, analysis of binary outcomes, and inverse probability weighting. </p><p>

Beyond her role as a statistician in SCRI, Ms. Nabilah is also involved in business development and leads webinar curation. She was awarded the National University Health System (NUHS) Commendation Award in 2021 for her outstanding contribution to NUHS’ services and strategic transformation agenda. She holds a BSc and MSc in Statistics from the National University of Singapore (NUS).
