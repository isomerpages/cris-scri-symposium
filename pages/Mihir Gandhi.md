	<h4><b>Dr Mihir Gandhi</b></h4>
<b>Head of Biostatistics<br>Singapore Clinical Research Institute (SCRI)</b>
	
<p>Dr Gandhi is a medical statistician with over 20 years of experience in clinical trial methodology, spanning both the pharmaceutical industry and academic research. </p><p>

He is currently the Head of Biostatistics unit at the Singapore Clinical Research Institute (SCRI) and Assistant Professor, Centre for Biomedical Data Science, Duke-NUS Medical School. </p><p>

As an expert in the design and analysis of large-scale, multi-country clinical trials, he has provided statistical guidance for diverse research portfolios, including Phase I to III randomized controlled trials, cluster randomized trials, and longitudinal cohort studies. In addition, he has methodological expertise in the development, validation, and health state valuation of patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs). </p><p>

Beyond his technical work, he is a dedicated educator and regularly teaches on clinical trial study design and applied biostatistics for doctoral and master's programs. He has published over 100 peer-reviewed journal articles with more than 4,000 citations and has been ranked as a Top 2% Scientist by citation impact in 2023 and 2024. </p><p>

He has also been recognized as a Chartered Statistician by the Royal Statistical Society, UK, and a Chartered Scientist by the Science Council, UK.

</p>
