	<h4><b>Dr Liang Shen</b></h4>
<b>Deputy Director, Biostatistics<br>Singapore Clinical Research Institute (SCRI)</b>
	
<p>Dr Shen Liang is the Deputy Director of Biostatistics at SCRI.</p><p>

With more than two decades of experience spanning theoretical and applied statistics, Dr Shen advances clinical research and healthcare innovation through rigorous methodology, collaborative leadership, and strategic guidance.</p><p>

Dr Shen earned her PhD in Statistics from the National University of Singapore (NUS), where her doctoral work focused on generalized linear and additive models with weighted distributions. She also holds a Master of Science in Statistics (by research) from NUS and a Master of Science in Stochastic Processes from East China Normal University, establishing a strong foundation in probability, stochastic analysis, and advanced statistical theory.</p><p>

Prior to her current role, Dr Shen led the Biostatistics Unit at the Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine, NUS, where she supervised a team of biostatisticians and provided expert consultation to medical researchers. Her work spanned the design and analysis of clinical trials across all phases, epidemiological studies, and real-world evidence research. </p><p>

A committed collaborator, she bridges statistical theory and clinical practice to ensure robust and reliable research outcomes. She has co-authored more than 200 peer-reviewed publications and is an experienced educator, having conducted biostatistics workshops and served as the principal lecturer and module coordinator for core biostatistics courses in the PhD and MSc programs.</p><p>

In addition to her academic and institutional leadership, Dr Shen serves as a statistical advisor and member of the Medical Advisory Committee and Product Vigilance Advisory Committee at Singapore’s Health Sciences Authority (HSA).
</p>
