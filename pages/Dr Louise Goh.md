	<h4><b>Dr Louise Goh</b></h4>
<b>Lead Specialist<br>Agency for Care Effectiveness (ACE)<br>Ministry of Health (Singapore)</b>
	
<p>Dr Louise Goh is a Lead Specialist at the Agency for Care Effectiveness. She is involved in managing the Utilisation Review and Outcomes Evaluation team which tracks utilisation and outcomes related to health technologies to assess the real-world impact of funding decisions. Louise obtained her Doctor of Philosophy in Public Health and was an academic at Curtin University prior to joining ACE.</p><p>
