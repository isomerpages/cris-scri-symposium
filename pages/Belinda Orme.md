	<h4><b>Dr Belinda Orme</b></h4>
<b>Director, International Training<br>TreeAge Software</b>
	
<p>Dr Belinda Orme has been a trainer for TreeAge Software for over 10 years, teaching many Health Economic TreeAge courses across Australasia and Europe. In addition to her training role, she supports users of the software in her day-to-day job alongside working with product development on new features of the software.</p><p>
She has a background in Mathematics and Applied Modelling from the University of Birmingham, UK (PhD). She specialised in Biofluid Dynamics. Prior to working with TreeAge, Belinda has a variety of experience across commercial and not-for-profit businesses as a Business Development Manager, and as a Scientist and Modeller in her post-graduate career. She has worked in the UK, Australia and US. 

</p>
