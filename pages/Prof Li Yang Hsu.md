	<h4><b>Prof Li Yang Hsu</b></h4>
<b>Professor, Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health<br>National University of Singapore</b>
	
<p>Dr Hsu is an infectious diseases physician who is the Director of the Asia Centre for Health Security at the Saw Swee Hock School of Public Health (SSHSPH), National University of Singapore (NUS) and administrative head of the newly-launched World Health Organization (WHO) Collaborating Centre in Clinical Trials which is based at the Advancing Clinical Evidence in Infectious Diseases (ADVANCE-ID) Network, NUS. He is also a member of the WHO Technical Advisory Group on Clinical Research Ecosystem Strengthening.
</p>
