	<h4><b>Dr Julie Zhu</b></h4>
<b>Senior Epidemiologist<br>Singapore Clinical Research Institute (SCRI)</b>
	
<p>Dr Julie Zhu is a senior epidemiologist in HEOR team, Singapore Clinical Research Institute. She is also a member of Cochrane Singapore. Her current work primarily involves health services research, evidence-based medicine and health economic evaluation across a wide range of disease areas, including infectious disease, oncology, metabolic disorders etc. Dr Julie has co-authored in about 20 scientific publications. </p><p>
In addition to academic work, Dr Julie has been teaching in various workshops. For example, Cochrane Systematic Review Development Workshop, Cochrane Workshop on Network Meta-analysis, Introduction to non-RCT study designs, Workshop on Clinical Practice Guideline development, Understanding and Interpretation of Health Economic Evaluation modelling, R software hands-on etc.</p><p>
Prior to joining SCRI, Julie worked as a research fellow at Singapore Institute of Clinical Sciences, A*STAR. Her research was in women and children’s health, working with data from the Growing Up in Singapore Towards healthy Outcomes (GUSTO) study cohort.

</p>
