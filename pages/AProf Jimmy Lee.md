	<h4><b>A/Prof Jimmy Lee</b></h4>
<b>Group Chief Research &amp; Innovation Officer<br>
Group Research &amp; Innovation<br>

<p>A/Prof Jimmy Lee is a Psychiatrist and Clinician-Scientist at the Institute of Mental Health, Singapore. He serves as the Group Chief Research &amp; Innovation Officer and Clinical Director of the Translational Research Office at NHG Health's Group Research and Innovation, where he leads NHG Health's precision medicine research strategy. He holds a concurrent appointment as Associate Professor and Director of the Academic Partnership Office at LKCMedicine, NTU.</p>
