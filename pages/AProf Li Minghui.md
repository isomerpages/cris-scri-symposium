	<h4><b>Assoc Prof Li Minghui</b></h4>
<b>Associate Professor<br>University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Pharmacy</b>
	
<p>Prof Li Minghui is an Associate Professor at the University of Tennessee Health Science Center College of Pharmacy. </p><p>

He earned his Ph.D. in Pharmaceutical and Health Outcomes from the University of South Carolina and completed a Fellowship in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) at the University of North Texas Health Science Center. </p><p>

Prof Li’s research focuses on real-world evidence (RWE), health policy, and health economics to support clinical and policy decision-making. He has extensive experience leveraging RWE and applying AI/ML to generate scientific evidence across diverse racial and ethnic populations. He has contributed to more than 20 funded research grants from prestigious agencies, including the National Institutes of Health (NIH). </p><p>

Prof Li has authored over 100 peer-reviewed publications in leading scientific journals, including *The Lancet*. His work has been recognized with more than 10 research awards, including Best Paper Awards from the *Journal of Managed Care &amp; Specialty Pharmacy*. His research findings have also been featured in major media outlets such as *The New York Times*.
</p>
