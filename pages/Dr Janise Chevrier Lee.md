	<h4><b>Dr Janise Chevrier-Lee</b></h4>
<b>Country Medical Head, Singapore<br>
Roche Singapore</b>
	
<p>Dr Janise Chevrier-Lee serves as the Country Medical Head for Roche Singapore, overseeing a broad portfolio including Medical Affairs, Clinical Operations, Compliance, Quality Assurance, and Regulatory Affairs. With deep experience in oncology, she advances innovative medicines through strong clinical leadership and evidence generation. A licensed physician and Singapore General Hospital alumna, Janise transitioned to the pharmaceutical industry nearly a decade ago. She holds an MBA from Chicago Booth and has built a robust foundation leading oncology launches and medical affairs across the APAC, Greater China, Middle East, and LATM regions at companies while at Astellas and Bayer. Dr Chevrier-Lee leverages her synergy of clinical insight and strategic leadership to champion patient-centric healthcare, fostering essential scientific partnerships that drive innovation across therapeutic areas.
</p>
