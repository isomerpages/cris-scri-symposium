	<h4><b>Prof Ching Lung Cheung</b></h4>
<b>Director, Real‑World Study and Application Centre (RWSAC)<br>
Greater Bay Area International Clinical Trial Institute (GBAICTI), Hong Kong</b>
	
<p>Prof Cheung is the director of the Real-World Study and Application Centre and an Associate Professor at HKU's Department of Pharmacology and Pharmacy, and President of the Osteoporosis Society of Hong Kong. He completed his PhD at HKU and post-doctoral training at Harvard Medical School and HKU, specialising in osteoporosis epidemiology and real-world study. Prof Cheung serves as associate editor for Osteoporosis and Sarcopenia, holds fellowship and committee roles with American Society of Bone and Mineral Research, and has published over 200 articles in leading journals such as Lancet, JAMA, and Nature Genetics.
</p>
