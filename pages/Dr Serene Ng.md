	<h4><b>Dr Serene Ng</b></h4>
<b>Chief Operating Officer<br>
	Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine (LKCMedicine)<br>
	Nanyang Technological University</b>
	
<p>Dr Serene Ng Boon Ching is the Chief Operating Officer of the Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine (LKCMedicine), Nanyang Technological University. She leads strategic initiatives that drive institutional growth, innovation, and operational excellence in support of the School’s mission in education, research, and healthcare.</p><p> 

Serene oversees advancement, governance, finance, corporate services, campus operations, as well as organisational transformation to support the School's mission in education, research and healthcare. She has led major initiatives in sustainability, digital transformation, cybersecurity governance, and workforce well‑being; resulting in LKCMedicine receiving national and regional awards including the Eco‑Office 4‑Leaves (Elite) Award, Grand Gold Award at CASE Asia Pacific Circle of Excellence Award, Recognition of Excellence on ADEPT Office (2025 &amp; 2026) on IT transformation and SBR Management Excellence Award for Health &amp; Wellness Initiative of the Year (2023), Employee Engagement of the Year – Education (2025) and Team of the Year – Education (2025). 
</p>
